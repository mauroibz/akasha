The library is the home again

One screen you arrive at, one bar that finds and adds, one Journal that gives you something to do and shows what you did. The screens below are drawn in Akasha's own type and tokens with invented covers. Pick a theme to see the proposed presets on every screen.

2. One bar for finding and adding

The top field is a real input on every screen. It answers from your library first and the web second, reads a pasted link or ISBN, and keeps the two last resorts in its footer. Add and Import leave the navigation.

Add ▾ files it under the medium's default status and offers the others (Reading, Read with a score, To read) without leaving the bar.

On the Library the same field filters the grid as you type, so there is no second search box under the first.

Things waiting to be sorted after an import show as “2 to sort” in the greeting line, not as a badge on a navigation item.

3. Arrange: make it yours, in place

Arrange turns the Library itself into the editor: drag a showcase, resize it, remove it, or add one from the tray. The same panel orders your media and picks the theme.

4. Journal: something to do, then what you did

The Journal opens on one widget at a time and you switch between them: Every day, this week or this month with a light for each day you read or watched something; Play, the games that are spread over Insights and Home today; and Goal. Below it come the timeline and the patterns. Play needs no navigation slot. The three tabs in the drawing work.
